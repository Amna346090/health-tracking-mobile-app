Privacy Policy

SFLBiotrack · Last updated: July 14, 2026

SFLBiotrack ("we", "us", "the app") is a health and medication tracking service used by patients and their care staff. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Information we collect

When you use SFLBiotrack (via the mobile app or the staff web dashboard), we collect:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text).
  • Health profile information: date of birth, gender, home address, and relevant health conditions.
  • Health tracking data: weight, mood/feeling entries, medication schedules, and medication dose logs (taken, missed, or skipped).
  • Progress photos: images you or your care staff choose to upload to track visual progress over time.
  • Notification data: a device push-notification token, used only to deliver medication reminders.

How we use your information

  • To operate the core service: tracking medications, health logs, and progress photos.
  • To send medication reminders via push notification or email.
  • To allow authorized care staff to review and support your care plan.
  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure.

We do not sell your personal or health information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see your data

Your health data is visible to you, and to authorized staff/admin users of the care team using SFLBiotrack to support your treatment. Patients cannot see other patients' data.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored and processed using the following infrastructure providers, under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Database: Neon (PostgreSQL hosting)
  • Application server: Render
  • Photo storage: Cloudflare R2
  • Email delivery: Resend

Passwords are never stored in plain text. Connections to our servers are encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Data retention and deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us using the details below; we will process such requests within a reasonable time, subject to any legal or record-keeping requirements that may apply to health data in your jurisdiction.

Children's privacy

SFLBiotrack is intended for use by adults and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
